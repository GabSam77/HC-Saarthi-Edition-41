HC SAARTHI — EDITION 17

WHAT CHANGED
- The original HDFC Capital logo supplied in the HDFC Capital concept presentation is restored to the top-left of every page, alongside SAARTHI.
- My Portfolio > Risk now uses a redesigned institutional risk and decision report, with an executive readout, four metrics, allocation chart, geographic bars, risk heatmap, action register, editable stress scenario and technical/legal register.
- The downloaded risk report is a self-contained HTML file. It embeds its own styles and the HDFC Capital logo and does not require any asset folder. Open the downloaded report and choose Print > Save as PDF; landscape A4 styling is included.
- The in-portal report and the exported report share the same content and design rules. Interactive controls are omitted from the exported report.
- All metrics remain illustrative and client-entered; risk screening is a heuristic, not a rating or probability of loss.

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip the package, keeping the folder intact.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Open My Portfolio > Risk.
4. Use Download full report to obtain a self-contained HTML file. Print / Save PDF opens the report for printing.
5. Do not use the prototype for sensitive client records without proper security and access controls.

The original corporate logo is a PNG from the supplied HDFC Capital concept presentation (591 x 265 pixels), not a vector artwork. It is displayed at a small size for clarity. Request the approved vector master for production branding.
